Why nexChat

Customer conversations shouldn't be a second full-time job.

Small businesses run on conversations, but answering the same questions, tracking orders across chats and remembering follow-ups takes hours every week.

Without nexChat

  • Answering the same questions about price, sizes, availability and delivery again and again.
  • Conversations scattered across threads, with no single view of each customer.
  • Orders written down by hand and payment status checked one message at a time.
  • Follow-ups forgotten, and customers left waiting outside business hours.

With nexChat

  • Common questions answered from your own products, policies and FAQs.
  • Each customer's conversation and order history in one place.
  • Carts and orders created from the conversation, with payment status you can track.
  • You stay in control: review, step in, or take over any conversation.
Features

Everything a conversation needs to become a completed task.

nexChat connects AI-assisted replies to the parts of your business customers actually ask about.

AI-powered conversations

Understands what customers are asking and replies naturally, using your business information.

Product catalog & discovery

Manage products, variants, prices and stock, and help customers find the right item in the chat.

Cart & order management

Build carts and create orders from the conversation, then track them from your dashboard.

Customer & conversation history

See past messages, orders and preferences for every customer before you reply.

Payment instructions & status

Share your payment instructions and keep track of which orders are paid, pending or cancelled.

Business knowledge & FAQs

Teach nexChat your opening hours, delivery areas, return policy and other answers you give often.

Human takeover & seller control

Pause the AI, step into any conversation, and decide what nexChat can and can't do on your behalf.

More workflows over time

Designed to grow beyond commerce into workflows such as appointment booking and reservations as they become available.

How it works

From message to action, in four steps.

nexChat sits between your customers and your business data, so each request is answered and, where it makes sense, acted on.

  1. 1

    A customer sends a message

    A customer asks a question, looks for a product, or wants to place an order.

    "Is the blue shirt available in M?"
  2. 2

    AI understands the request

    nexChat works out what the customer wants, using your catalog and business knowledge.

    intent: check availability
    product: Linen Shirt · M
  3. 3

    nexChat takes the right action

    It looks up stock, updates a cart, creates an order or shares payment instructions, within the limits you set.

    stock check → in stock
  4. 4

    Everyone gets a clear response

    The customer gets an answer, and your team sees the conversation and any orders in the dashboard.

    reply sent · order #1042 logged
