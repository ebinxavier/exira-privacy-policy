Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our platform collects, uses, and safeguards information when integrating with Meta Platforms, Inc. and the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API).

1. Data We Process

  • Account Identifiers: Meta User IDs, WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) IDs, Phone Number IDs, and OAuth access tokens.
  • Messaging Payloads: Inbound and outbound message text, timestamps, and delivery/read receipts.
  • Diagnostic Logs: System event metadata and webhook verification tokens.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Data is used exclusively to:

  • Automate inbound and outbound customer support messages.
  • Route webhook notifications to appropriate tenant dashboards.
  • Maintain API connectivity with Meta's Graph API.
We do not sell, license, or monetize customer communication logs or personal data to third parties or marketing platforms.

3. Data Security & Storage

Tokens and credentials are securely stored using industry-standard encryption protocols. Message payloads are maintained only as long as necessary to process automated conversational logic.

User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Developer Data Policies and international privacy regulations, you have the right to request deletion of all stored data associated with your account.

To request deletion:

  • Email your request to support@example.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your Phone Number ID.
  • Or navigate to your Facebook Account Settings & Privacy > Settings > Business Integrations and disconnect our app.

All associated tokens and cached logs will be permanently deleted within 48 hours of confirmation.
